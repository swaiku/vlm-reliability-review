All 40 sources were read at abstract / project-page level only (no full text yet). Claims below come from those pages and must be checked in the full text before they appear in the paper.

Relevance scale: **Core** = directly measures reliability of VLMs (or the compared detectors) on images for our question; **Supporting** = explains a mechanism or gives context; **Background** = describes a model or method we build on; **Peripheral** = tangential, first to drop if space is short.
